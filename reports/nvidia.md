# NVIDIA research lab — first financial comparison

Research cutoff: 2026-09-26. GAAP, consolidated, USD. FY2024–FY2026 annual comparatives from the FY2026 10-K, published February 25, 2026. These are not calendar years or historical point-in-time estimates. Later quarterly results are outside this first milestone.

| Fiscal year | Period end | Revenue ($bn) | Revenue YoY | Operating income ($bn) | Operating margin | Operating cash flow ($bn) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FY2024 | 2024-01-28 | 60.922 | — | 32.972 | 54.12% | 28.090 |
| FY2025 | 2025-01-26 | 130.497 | 114.20% | 81.453 | 62.42% | 64.089 |
| FY2026 | 2026-01-25 | 215.938 | 65.47% | 130.387 | 60.38% | 102.718 |

Financial evidence: [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm), Consolidated Statements of Income and Cash Flows.

## Calculations

Operating margin = operating income / revenue × 100. Revenue YoY = (current revenue / previous revenue − 1) × 100. The first year has no growth figure because the comparison baseline is outside this dataset.

## Management's explanations

Manually reviewed paraphrases, attributed to management; not independently established causal conclusions.

- **FY2025:** Management attributes growth to demand for Hopper-based AI computing. A larger Data Center revenue share improved gross margin; workforce and development spending increased operating expenses. Higher revenue supported operating cash flow. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000023/nvda-20250126.htm). Location: Item 7: Fiscal Year 2025 Summary; Gross Profit and Gross Margin; Liquidity and Capital Resources.
- **FY2026:** Management links revenue growth to AI infrastructure demand. The Blackwell systems transition and H20 inventory charges reduced gross margin. Compensation and infrastructure spending increased operating expenses. Management attributes higher operating cash flow to higher revenue. [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm). Location: Item 7: Fiscal Year 2026 Summary; Gross Profit and Gross Margin; Liquidity and Capital Resources.

## What we can conclude

Revenue and operating cash flow increased in both comparisons. Operating margin rose in FY2025 and fell in FY2026. This describes reported business performance; it does not estimate stock value or predict returns.
