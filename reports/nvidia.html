Management's explanations

Manually reviewed paraphrases, attributed to management; these are not independently established causal conclusions.

FY2025

Management attributes growth to demand for Hopper-based AI computing. A larger Data Center revenue share improved gross margin; workforce and development spending increased operating expenses. Higher revenue supported operating cash flow.

FY2026

Management links revenue growth to AI infrastructure demand. The Blackwell systems transition and H20 inventory charges reduced gross margin. Compensation and infrastructure spending increased operating expenses. Management attributes higher operating cash flow to higher revenue.
